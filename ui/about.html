About fireside

Last updated: 9 November 2026

fireside turns a book or a web story into audio you can play, pause, resume and keep. Every chapter is rendered to a finished audio file before it plays, so playback does not stall partway the way a live read-aloud does. Close the tab, come back a week later, and it carries on where you stopped.

What it does

  • Bring your own books. An EPUB from your shelf, or a link to a story on the web. Both land on the same shelf.
  • Pick the voice. Connect your own speech key, or buy narration from us by the character.
  • Keep it. A chapter is voiced once and stored, so listening to it again costs nothing.

How the money works

Narration is the cost. We buy it from a speech provider and charge the provider's rate plus a small margin that covers the payment fee and support, currently $19.63 per million characters, about fifteen hours of listening. If you connect your own speech key, you pay the provider directly and pay us nothing for narration. The hosted service is run by __COMPANY__; the software itself is open source, and you can run your own server and pay nobody for it.
