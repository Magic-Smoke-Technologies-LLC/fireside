Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

fireside reads books and web stories aloud. This policy explains what the hosted service at readfireside.com and the fireside mobile app collect, why, and what you can do about it. If you run fireside on your own server, you are the operator of that server and this policy describes what the software does with your data; you are responsible for it.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and a password, or the identity returned by a sign-in provider you choose (Google or SimpleLogin). Passwords are stored only as a salted hash; we never see the plain text.
  • Library content. The books and stories you add, the voices and covers you generate, and your playback position, so your shelf is where you left it.
  • Session data. A session identifier, your browser or device user-agent, IP address, and last-seen time, so you can see and revoke signed-in devices.
  • Billing records. Your plan, the narration characters included with it each period, your prepaid narration credit balance, and purchase history. Card numbers are handled by our payment processor and never reach our servers.
  • The text you ask us to read. To turn text into speech we send it to a speech provider. That is Microsoft Azure when you use our hosted voice service; when you connect your own key, the text goes to your provider under your own account.

Why we are allowed to use it

  • Running your account and reading your books (contract). Your email, password, library, playback position, and the text we send to a speech provider, because that is what you asked us to do.
  • Taking payment and keeping records (contract and legal obligation). Plan, credit balance, purchase history, and invoices. Tax and accounting law requires us to keep some of it.
  • Keeping the service safe and working (legitimate interests). Session identifiers, device and IP address, and server logs, to keep you signed in, stop abuse, and fix breakage. We keep this to what the job needs, and you can object (below).
  • Measuring how the site is used (consent). Only if you turn it on. Off means off: nothing is sent to Google Analytics before you tick the box, and unticking it stops any further measurement.

We do not use your library or your text to train models, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have a legal or similarly significant effect.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run advertising or third-party ad trackers.
  • We do not use your library to train models.

Cookies and local storage

One cookie is strictly necessary: the session cookie that keeps you signed in. Your browser's local storage holds small preferences such as your theme and whether you turned analytics on. No advertising cookies are set by us or for us.

Analytics, only with your consent. Analytics is off by default. Nothing is sent to Google Analytics until you turn it on in Account settings, and turning it off stops any further measurement. When it is on, Google receives the page you are on, your browser type, and a coarse location derived from your IP address; Google's own privacy policy and its opt-out add-on apply. We keep that measurement for up to 14 months.

Who we share data with

  • Cloud hosting (Microsoft Azure) — where the service and database run.
  • Speech synthesis — Microsoft Azure, or the provider you connect yourself, receives the text being read.
  • Payments — Paddle (paddle.com) processes purchases as merchant of record. It receives your email address and order details, and it handles your card data; we never see it.
  • Sign-in — Google or SimpleLogin, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Email delivery — a transactional mail provider sends password-reset and account messages.

We share only what each of these needs to do its job, and only when you use that feature. Each provider that touches personal data on our behalf is bound by a data processing agreement. Paddle is the merchant of record for purchases: it acts as the controller of your payment and billing data as well as processing it for us, and its own privacy policy governs that part.

Where your data goes

Our hosting, database and speech provider are in the United States (Microsoft Azure), our payment provider is in the United Kingdom (Paddle), and analytics, if you enable it, is in the United States (Google). Transfers out of the EEA, the UK or Switzerland rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where a provider is certified, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK extension to it. You can ask us for the safeguards we rely on.

Keeping your data

  • Account and library. While your account is open. When you delete it, we remove your library, sessions, rendered audio, and account record, and any backup copy ages out within 30 days.
  • Server logs. Up to 30 days, for security and debugging.
  • Billing records. For as long as tax and accounting law requires, which is currently seven years. These records are kept even after you close your account, because the law does not let us drop them.
  • Analytics. Up to 14 months, and only if you turned it on.

Your rights

You can ask us to do any of these, and we do not charge for it:

  • See and copy the personal data we hold about you, and take it with you in a machine-readable file.
  • Correct anything wrong, and complete anything incomplete.
  • Delete your account and your data. You can do this yourself from Settings > Account, or by asking us. How to delete your account →
  • Restrict or object to a use of your data. You can object to anything we do on legitimate interests, including the security logs, and we stop unless we have a compelling reason the law recognises.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for analytics, from Account settings. That does not affect anything done while it was on.
  • Complain to a data protection authority. In the EEA, that is the authority in the country where you live or work, or where the alleged breach happened (the list of members); in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

Write to __CONTACT__. We answer within one month. If a request is complex we may take up to three months and will tell you why. We may ask you to confirm you are the account holder, and we answer to the email on the account.

Other choices

  • Revoke devices. Sign out individual devices or all others from Settings.
  • Forget a book or voice. Remove any book or voice from your shelf at any time.

Children

fireside is not directed at children. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16; elsewhere the line is 13. If you believe a child has given us their data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit, passwords are hashed, and sessions are individually revocable. No service is perfectly secure, but we try to hold the line here. If a breach affects your data and puts you at risk, we tell you without undue delay and, where the law requires it, the supervisory authority within 72 hours.
