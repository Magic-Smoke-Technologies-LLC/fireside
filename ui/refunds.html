Refund policy

If the fault is ours, you get your money or your credit back. That is the whole policy. Payments are handled by Paddle as merchant of record, so a refund of a Paddle payment may be issued through Paddle.

When we refund

  • A render that failed or came out wrong because of us. If a book or chapter will not voice, half the audio is missing, playback is broken, or the narration charged you for something we did not deliver, we refund that charge or restore the credit it cost. You do not need to prove anything beyond telling us what happened.
  • An outage that cost you paid time or credit. Same answer: restore the credit, refund the period, or extend it, whichever makes you whole.
  • A charge you did not mean to make. A duplicate or surprise charge on your account is ours to sort out, so tell us and we will refund it.
  • Where the law says you can cancel. Consumer law in some countries gives you a right to cancel or to a refund that we cannot exclude. Where that applies to you, that right stands even if it is wider than this page.

When we do not

  • A change of mind after narration you asked for has already been rendered and paid for on your behalf. The speech has been bought from the speech provider by then.
  • Spend through your own speech provider key. That is your account with them, under their terms, not a charge from us.
  • A file or link the service was not able to read because of how it arrived, if we told you so and charged you nothing.

Cancelling is not a refund

  • Cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps your access to the end of the period you already paid for. Cancel in the billing panel in Account, or write to us.
  • If you cancel and something on this page also applies, say so in the same message and we will handle both.

How to ask

  • Email __CONTACT__ with your account email and the date of the charge, plus one line on what went wrong. You do not need a formal claim.
  • We answer within a few days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, and how fast it lands depends on the bank or card issuer. Credit we restore appears in your balance immediately.
