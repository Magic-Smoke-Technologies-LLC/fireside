Buy narration separately — $19.63 per million characters

Characters bought on their own go into your balance, never expire, and are spent only on chapters you render. Each million is roughly 15 hours of listening. That price is the speech provider's rate plus about $0.10 per million, which covers the payment fee and support.

What you get, on every plan

  • Pay once per chapter, then listen for free, forever. A chapter you have already voicing stays voiced: replaying it, re-listening in a year, or coming back after a break costs no characters. You are charged for new text, not for listening.
  • Bring your own Azure Speech key and pay us nothing for narration. Your text goes to Microsoft under your own account and Azure bills you directly. Cheapest way to run a large library.
  • Import what you already have. An EPUB, or a link to a story page. Chapters split themselves and keep their order.
  • Follow along. The page and the voice stay together, with the current sentence highlighted as it is read.
  • Say names right. A pronunciation dictionary per book or for your whole library, so a character's name is not mangled for 300 pages.
  • Resume anywhere. Your position follows you between the browser and the installed app, per book.
  • Open source. Run the server yourself and pay nobody for the software.

Run it yourself — free

fireside is open-source software. Run the server on your own machine, point it at your own speech provider key, and you pay nobody for the software.
