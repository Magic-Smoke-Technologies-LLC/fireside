    Default voice

    The voice a new conversion starts on, and the voice the sandbox opens on. A book already voiced keeps the voice it has until you convert it again.

    Each source asks for what it needs (AO3 asks for a cookie). These live on the server, never in this browser.

    Reading along

    While a chapter plays, the page marks the sentence being read and can follow it down the page. Timing is estimated from how long the chapter takes to say, so a long pause or a scene break can put it a sentence off. It never changes the audio.

    Saved to your account, so it follows you to the phone.

    Pronunciations

    Words and symbols the voice says wrong. Left: exactly what appears in the text. Right: how it should sound, spelled out (Qui-Gon → Kwai-Gon, ʛ → galleon). Works when the symbol is glued to a number too: 50ʛ and ʛ50,000 both read right.

    Scope: all books, one author, or one book. A writer's own money glyphs usually belong to the author; a single story's belong to the book. The same word in a narrower scope wins.

    Numbers written with commas (50,000) are read as words (fifty thousand) automatically. A plain 1999 or Chapter 12 is left as the author wrote it.

    Language

    Display

    Contrast and colour. Stored in this browser, so each device can differ.

    High contrast uses white on black with thicker borders, and the status marks are told apart by shape as well as colour.

    Colour-vision safe keeps the dark background and moves the status colours to blue and purple, which stay apart for the common kinds of colour blindness. Like every theme, the marks also differ in shape.

    A plan buys the reader

    This server, the app, your shelf and your storage. Import an EPUB or a story link, split it into chapters, follow along as it reads, resume on any device.

    The voice is metered separately

    Three ways: bring your own Azure speech key, run Kokoro (or any OpenAI-compatible speech server) on your own machine and connect it in Settings, or add dollars to your wallet and our voice reads to you at the standard rate below. A paid plan already includes some wallet credit each month.

    Your wallet

    Wallet dollars never expire and are used to pay for services on the platform.

    Our rate is the speech provider's rate plus about ten cents per million, which covers the payment fee and support. The rate can change if the provider's does, and the page always shows the current one.

    On every plan

    • Pay once per chapter, then listen for free, forever. A chapter you have already voiced stays voiced; replaying it next year costs no characters. You are charged for new text, not for listening.
    • Import what you already have. An EPUB, or a link to a story page. Chapters split themselves and keep their order.
    • Follow along. The page and the voice stay together, with the current sentence highlighted as it is read.
    • Say names right. A pronunciation dictionary per book or for your whole library.
    • Resume anywhere. Your position follows you between the browser and the installed app, per book.

    Run it yourself, free

    fireside is open-source software. Run the server on your own machine, point it at your own speech provider key, and you pay nobody for the software.
